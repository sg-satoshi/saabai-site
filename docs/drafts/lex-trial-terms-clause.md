**Free trial: who may use it, and how**

1. The Lex free trial is for Australian legal practices assessing Lex for their own legal work.
2. You must not use the trial, or let anyone else use it, if you are, or act for, a business that makes, sells or is developing legal software or legal AI products. You must not use it for benchmarking, competitive analysis or market research about Lex.
3. You must not copy, reproduce, record or publish Lex's interface, outputs, prompts, workflows or documentation for any purpose other than your firm's own legal work. You must not scrape, crawl or extract data from Lex by automated means. You must not reverse engineer, decompile or try to find out how Lex works internally, or get around any limit or security measure.
4. Trial details must be true and current, including your name, firm, work email, phone number and address. One free trial per firm.
5. We may suspend or end a free trial at any time, for any reason, with or without notice, and without liability. This includes when we reasonably believe this clause has been breached.
6. Nothing in this clause limits any other rights we have under these Terms or at law.
