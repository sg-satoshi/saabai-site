About InkFX

A family business built on prints worth keeping.

InkFX Printing started as a simple belief: every photograph deserves to be printed with the same care the photographer put into capturing it. From our Gold Coast studio, we've spent over a decade obsessing over colour accuracy, substrate selection, and the tiny details that separate an average print from an extraordinary one.

We work with photographers, interior designers, artists, and everyday people who want something they're proud to put on a wall. Whether it's a single paper print or a full canvas collection, we treat every order as if it's the only one we're printing today.

Our HD Metal prints have won multiple industry awards, but what we're most proud of is the photographers who come back to us, order after order, because they know they can trust the result.
