Can I use the voucher at either location? +
Yes. Our gift vouchers are fully redeemable at both our Worongary studio (23 Mudgeeraba Rd) and our Southport studio (1A/5 Olympic Cct). Simply mention the voucher code when booking.
How long is the voucher valid for? +
All gift vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. Unused vouchers can be extended on request.
Can I send the voucher directly to someone? +
Absolutely. During checkout you can enter the recipient's name and email address. We will send a personalised gift voucher directly to them.
Can the voucher be used for any treatment? +
Yes. Vouchers can be applied toward any treatment at either studio including Relaxation Massage, Deep Tissue, Hot Stone, and the Heaven Thai Signature. If the treatment costs more than the voucher, just pay the difference on the day.
What if I lose the voucher? +
No problem. Just contact us and we can resend the voucher. We keep a record of all purchases.
