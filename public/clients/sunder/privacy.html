Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how Saabai Pty Ltd (ABN 44 703 735 264), trading as SUNDER ("we", "us"), handles your personal information when you join the SUNDER waitlist at sunder.com.au. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

When you join the waitlist:

  • Your first name and email address.
  • Your mobile number, if you choose to give it. It is optional.
  • Referral details: your personal referral code, the code of whoever referred you (if any), and how many extra giveaway entries you have earned (up to 10).
  • How you found us: the page you signed up on and campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source).
  • A record of your consent: the wording you agreed to, its version and the time.
  • Basic technical data: your browser's user agent and a one-way hashed version of your IP address, used to prevent spam and abuse. We do not keep your raw IP address in the waitlist.

On the thank-you screen, if you choose:

  • Your SMS opt-in, with the wording you agreed to and the time.
  • Your answer to whether you would rather buy outright or subscribe, and when you answered.

We don't use advertising pixels or third-party analytics on the waitlist page. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

Why we collect it

  • To send you SUNDER launch updates and early access.
  • To give you the 20% launch offer.
  • To run the waitlist giveaway, including counting referral entries and contacting winners.
  • To send texts, but only if you tick the separate SMS box on the thank-you screen. Giving a mobile at signup is not consent to texts.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns work and what customers prefer at launch.
  • To protect the form from spam and abuse.

If you don't give us your name and email, you can't join the waitlist or enter the giveaway.

Unsubscribing

  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • SMS: reply STOP to any text.
  • Or contact us at hello@sunder.com.au.

We act on unsubscribe requests within 5 business days.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We use service providers to run the waitlist, including:

  • Supabase (database), where the waitlist is stored.
  • Vercel (website hosting). The site runs in Vercel's Sydney region.
  • Email and SMS sending providers, to send the messages you have agreed to receive.

Some of these providers are based overseas, including in the United States, and may access or store data outside Australia. We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect your information. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist details: while you stay subscribed. We delete or de-identify them within a reasonable time after you unsubscribe or the waitlist closes.
  • Mobile numbers without SMS opt-in: deleted 30 days after launch.
  • Consent and unsubscribe records: kept as long as needed to show we followed the Spam Act 2003.
  • Giveaway winner records: kept for as long as state trade promotion laws require.

Security

The waitlist database can only be read by our server, not from the public website. Access is limited to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Access and correction

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Email hello@sunder.com.au. We'll reply within 30 days, after checking your identity.

Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your information, email hello@sunder.com.au. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version will always be on this page.
