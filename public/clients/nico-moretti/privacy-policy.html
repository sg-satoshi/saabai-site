Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how Nico Moretti, based in Adelaide, South Australia, handles personal information collected on nicomoretti.au. It is written in plain English for the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), in particular APP 1 (an open and transparent policy), APP 5 (telling you when information is collected) and APP 8 (sending information outside Australia).

Saabai (saabai.ai) is the technology provider. Saabai runs this website, the chat server, the chat logs, the inquiry storage and the email notifications for Nico Moretti. Saabai's access is limited to operating the service.

The website does not publish an ABN, a phone number, or an email address. To ask about your information, use the inquiry form on the contact section of the website and say that your message is about privacy. A privacy request sent that way is kept only for as long as needed to handle the request.

The website chat

The site has a chat called Sofia. Sofia is an AI assistant. The replies are not typed by a person. The chat says this when it opens, and a short notice stays under the message box, including when an older chat is restored on your device.

When you send a message, this is what happens.

  • Your browser keeps the chat on this device, under the name nico-chat-history. It keeps the last 40 messages. That copy stays until you clear this site's saved data, or your browser removes it.
  • The chat kept in the browser, up to those 40 messages, is sent to this website's server with each new message. That server runs in Sydney, Australia, on Vercel.
  • The server sends those messages to DeepSeek so DeepSeek can write the reply. The call goes to api.deepseek.com and uses the model deepseek-v4-flash. DeepSeek is Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd. DeepSeek's Open Platform Terms of Service are governed by the law of the People's Republic of China. Those terms say DeepSeek's consumer privacy policy does not cover people who use a developer's app, and that the developer must explain how that information is handled. The terms do not name a storage country for API messages. DeepSeek is based in China and may process and store data there. Sending your messages there is a disclosure outside Australia under APP 8.

Do not type sensitive information into the chat. That includes passwords, payment card numbers, bank details, tax file numbers, identity document numbers, health information, sexual matters, and sexual preferences or practices. What you type is sent to DeepSeek, and DeepSeek is based in China and may process and store data there. A copy may also be stored in Japan, as described below.

What we store from the chat, and for how long

When the chat log database is connected, the server keeps a copy of your latest message and Sofia's reply in Supabase. That project is in Tokyo, Japan, which is outside Australia. The copy includes the text (shortened if it is longer than 20,000 characters), the time, the model name, the token counts, a cost figure for that reply, and labels for the bot (nico) and the site (nico-moretti). Each reply is stored as its own record. Earlier messages in the same chat are sent again to DeepSeek with your next message. They are not written again unless they were the latest message on an earlier reply. The chat record in Supabase does not include your IP address.

If that database is not connected, the chat still replies and no server copy of the messages is stored. The copy in your browser is separate.

A daily job is set to delete these chat records after 90 days.

Chat requests also store your IP address in Upstash Redis so the site can limit how many messages one address can send. That rate-limit record is kept for about 26 hours. A shorter counter, used when messages arrive in a burst, is kept for about 11 minutes. Upstash stores each database in a region chosen for that database. Upstash publishes regions in Sydney, the United States, Europe (including Ireland, Germany and the United Kingdom), Japan, Singapore, India, Brazil, South Africa and Canada. The region configured for this database could not be confirmed from the project settings available for this update, so the IP address may be stored in Australia or in one of those other countries.

The inquiry form

The contact form collects your name, email address, phone number if you give one, your message, the duration you select, and the event type. A short notice next to Submit says this before you send it. The form is sent to this website's inquiry service on saabai.ai.

The inquiry is saved in the same Upstash Redis service. It is kept until it is deleted. You can ask for deletion through the inquiry form. A privacy request is kept only for as long as needed to handle it. The region is the one described above, and it may be in Australia or outside Australia.

A copy is emailed to the business through Resend. Resend's published GDPR notice says Resend stores customer data in the United States, including message content. Choosing a sending region does not change that storage location.

The same inquiry is sent to Telegram at api.telegram.org. Telegram's privacy policy names its parent, Telegram Group Inc., and Telegraph Inc., both in the British Virgin Islands, and Telegram FZ-LLC in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Telegram says cloud chats are stored on its servers, with encryption keys held in several jurisdictions. Telegram says that if you signed up from the UK or the EEA, that account data is stored in the Netherlands. An inquiry sent to this Telegram bot is a cloud message, so it can be stored outside Australia.

The chat and the inquiry form are separate. Typing in the chat does not fill in the form.

Other services that receive a request

  • Vercel hosts the website and the server functions. The functions are set to run in Sydney. Vercel is a company in the United States. Vercel keeps request logs, which can include an IP address, the browser, and the page requested.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), including Material Symbols. Google is based in the United States. Your browser sends your IP address when it loads the fonts. Google may process that request in the United States or in other countries where Google operates.
  • Pages load the Tailwind script from cdn.tailwindcss.com. That address is served by Cloudflare and hosted on Vercel in the United States (Ashburn, Virginia). Your browser sends your IP address when it loads the script. The request can pass through a Cloudflare location closer to you.

Why we collect it

We collect this information to answer questions about Nico Moretti's services, to reply to an inquiry, to run the website chat, and to limit abuse of the chat. We do not sell personal information.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by using the inquiry form. Say that the message is about privacy, and include the email address you used so we can find the right record. We may need to check that you are the person the information is about before we release it. A privacy request made this way is kept only for as long as needed to handle it.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, contact us the same way first. If you are not satisfied with the response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC):
