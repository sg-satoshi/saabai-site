Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how Nico Moretti, based in Adelaide, South Australia, handles personal information collected on nicomoretti.au. It is written in plain English for the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), in particular APP 1 (an open and transparent policy), APP 5 (telling you when information is collected) and APP 8 (sending information outside Australia).

Saabai (saabai.ai) is the technology provider. Saabai runs this website, the chat server, the chat logs, the inquiry storage and the email notifications for Nico Moretti. Saabai, as the site's developer, can access leads and chats to run and support the site.

The website does not publish an ABN, a phone number, or an email address. To ask about your information, use the inquiry form on the contact section of the website and say that your message is about privacy. Enquiries and privacy requests are kept until Nico deletes them or you ask for them to be deleted. Copies also exist in the email and Telegram services named in this policy.

The website chat

The site has a chat called Sofia. Sofia is an AI assistant. The replies are not typed by a person. The first message in the chat says this. Under the message box, the chat says it is powered by Saabai. It can also show a short line asking you not to share sensitive information. A small link labelled Privacy policy opens this policy.

When you send a message, this is what happens.

  • Your browser keeps this chat for the tab, under the name saabai-chat:nico-moretti. That copy holds the conversation id, the time, and the last 40 messages. It stays until you close the tab, the chat has been idle for 30 minutes, or you clear this site's saved data.
  • The browser also keeps three small markers for this site. They are not the chat text. saabai-chat:nico-moretti:seen means the chat has loaded. saabai-chat:nico-moretti:nd is a list of times a prompt to open the chat was shown. saabai-chat:nico-moretti:rv means a review link was offered. Those stay until you clear this site's saved data.
  • If this browser still has an older copy named nico-chat-history, the chat deletes it when it loads.
  • The chat kept in the browser, up to those 40 messages, is sent to this website's server with each new message, together with the conversation id. That server runs in Sydney, Australia, on Vercel.
  • The server sends those messages to DeepSeek so DeepSeek can write the reply. The call goes to api.deepseek.com and uses the model deepseek-v4-flash. DeepSeek is Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd. DeepSeek's Open Platform Terms of Service are governed by the law of the People's Republic of China. Those terms say DeepSeek's consumer privacy policy does not cover people who use a developer's app, and that the developer must explain how that information is handled. The terms do not name a storage country for API messages. DeepSeek is based in China and may process and store data there. Sending your messages there is a disclosure outside Australia under APP 8.
  • If DeepSeek returns an error, or does not answer within about 12 seconds, the server stops that call and sends the same messages to Anthropic so Anthropic can write the reply. That call goes to api.anthropic.com and uses Claude Haiku 4.5 (model id claude-haiku-4-5). That fallback is set in the site code for this chat. Anthropic, PBC is based in the United States and processes these API requests there. Sending your messages there is also a disclosure outside Australia under APP 8.

Do not type sensitive information into the chat. That includes passwords, payment card numbers, bank details, tax file numbers, identity document numbers, health information, sexual matters, and sexual preferences or practices. What you type is sent to DeepSeek, and if DeepSeek does not answer, to Anthropic in the United States. A copy may also be stored in Japan, as described below.

What we store from the chat, and for how long

A copy of each chat is kept in Supabase, in Tokyo, Japan, for 90 days. The copy includes your latest message and Sofia's reply. Text longer than 20,000 characters is shortened. The record also includes the time, the model name (including which provider answered), the token counts, a cost figure for that reply, a conversation id, and labels for the bot and the site. New messages are labelled nico-moretti for both. Older messages may still be labelled nico. They are not moved. Each reply is stored as its own record. Messages with the same conversation id are one chat. Earlier messages in the same chat are sent again with your next message, to DeepSeek or to Anthropic if DeepSeek does not answer. They are not written again unless they were the latest message on an earlier reply. That chat record does not include your raw IP address. When the browser sends a conversation id, that id is a random value from the browser. When it does not, the server makes one with a SHA-256 hash of the IP address, the browser, the date, and the site session secret already used to sign signed-in sessions. The hash is shortened. It does not contain the raw IP address or the secret. The same id is stored with the chat in Supabase, and it is also stored in an Upstash Redis set for 70 days so the month's conversation count can be worked out.

A chat is counted as a lead when you send more than a greeting. A greeting on its own is not counted. The lead is a count of those chats for the month. It is worked out from the stored messages and the conversation id. It is not a copy of the inquiry form, and typing in the chat does not fill in the form. A button in the chat can open the enquiry form at https://nicomoretti.au/#contact. Sending that form is a separate step, described below.

Upstash Redis holds more than the rate limit. The conversation id sits in a set for 70 days. Counts of chat events, and the token counts for replies, are kept for 400 days. A log of website addresses that were blocked from the chat is kept as a count for each site and address. No expiry is set on that log.

Chat requests also store your IP address in Upstash Redis so the site can limit how many messages one address can send. That daily rate-limit record is kept for about 26 hours. A shorter counter, used when messages arrive in a burst, is kept for about 11 minutes. Those keys contain the IP address. Chat events, such as a button click, use a separate key that stores a hash of the IP address with the site session secret, not the raw IP address. That key is kept for about 90 seconds. Upstash stores each database in a region chosen for that database. Upstash publishes regions in Sydney, the United States, Europe (including Ireland, Germany and the United Kingdom), Japan, Singapore, India, Brazil, South Africa and Canada. The IP address may be stored in Australia or in one or more of those countries.

The inquiry form

The contact form collects your name, email address, phone number if you give one, your message, and the duration you select. The home page, the Husband Experience page, and the Brisbane, Canberra, Melbourne, Perth and Sydney pages also collect the event type. The seven city pages also send the city shown on that page. Gold Coast and Hobart do not ask for an event type. A short notice next to Submit says this before you send it. The form is sent to this website's inquiry service on saabai.ai.

The inquiry is saved in the same Upstash Redis service. There is no expiry on that record. Enquiries and privacy requests are kept until Nico deletes them or you ask for them to be deleted. Copies also exist in the email and Telegram services named in this policy. The saved inquiry may be stored in Australia or in one or more of those countries.

A copy is emailed to the business through Resend. Resend's published GDPR notice says Resend stores customer data in the United States, including message content. Choosing a sending region does not change that storage location.

The same inquiry is sent to Telegram at api.telegram.org. Telegram's privacy policy is published by Telegram Messenger Inc. It names Telegram Group Inc. and Telegraph Inc. in the British Virgin Islands, and Telegram FZ-LLC in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Telegram stores cloud messages on servers in several countries.

The chat and the inquiry form are separate. Typing in the chat does not fill in the form. Opening the enquiry form from the chat does not send the chat.

Other services that receive a request

  • Vercel hosts the website and the server functions. The functions are set to run in Sydney. Vercel is a company in the United States. Vercel keeps request logs, which can include an IP address, the browser, and the page requested.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), including Material Symbols. Google is based in the United States. Your browser sends your IP address when it loads the fonts. Google may process that request in the United States or in other countries where Google operates.
  • Pages load the Tailwind script from cdn.tailwindcss.com. That address is served through Cloudflare and Vercel, which may be outside Australia. Your browser sends your IP address when it loads the script.

Why we collect it

We collect this information to answer questions about Nico Moretti's services, to reply to an inquiry, to run the website chat, and to limit abuse of the chat. We do not sell personal information.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by using the inquiry form. Say that the message is about privacy, and include the email address you used so we can find the right record. We may need to check that you are the person the information is about before we release it. Enquiries and privacy requests are kept until Nico deletes them or you ask for them to be deleted. Copies also exist in the email and Telegram services named in this policy.
