Draft walks you through Setup questions, then writes the document. It is jurisdiction aware. Before use, the QA check runs (Checking every citation and clause) and lists References in this draft, each with Show in draft, Research this and Find on AustLII. Every draft is marked Must review before use. You can Edit answers to regenerate, Export to Word, or Send to client. Uploaded files are read to fill the draft and then discarded, and draft progress is saved only in your browser for 30 days.
