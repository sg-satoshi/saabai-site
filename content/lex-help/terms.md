Lex is a monthly subscription on the Firm plan, which includes 3 lawyer seats. Pricing is set after the trial, and you'll see the full price, GST and terms before you pay anything. If the price changes, Saabai emails you at least 30 days before it applies, and you can cancel before then. You can cancel anytime using the Manage billing link in your Lex account; there's no lock-in and no cancellation fee. Access continues until the end of the month already paid for. Part months aren't refunded, but if you're charged in error or Lex doesn't work as it should, contact Saabai and it will be put right. If a payment fails, you'll be told and it is retried; after 7 days access may be paused until it is paid. After a plan ends, matters and research chats are kept for 30 days, then deleted; you can ask for a copy or earlier deletion at hello@saabai.ai. Lex helps prepare drafts and research and doesn't give legal advice. The full terms are at saabai.ai/terms (section 10) and the privacy policy at saabai.ai/privacy.
