Review a document checks an uploaded document and lists issues for a lawyer to consider. Compare shows the differences between two versions. Your firm has a daily cap on reviews and compares; when it is reached Lex says so and the limit resets at midnight Brisbane time. A review can be sent to a supervisor and saved to a matter.
