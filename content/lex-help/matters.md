Matters keep a client's research, drafts and reviews together for your firm. Use New matter and fill in Matter name, Client name, Matter type, Area, Brief description, Other parties and aliases, Notes and deadlines. Open matters shows Next deadline and Overdue items. Inside a matter, New research in this matter, Draft a document and Review a document all save there. Leave matter (Stop working in this matter) returns to general work. Adding other parties and aliases helps Lex mask those names before anything goes to the AI model.
