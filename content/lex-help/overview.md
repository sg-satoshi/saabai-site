Lex is Saabai's AI workspace for Australian law firms. The left sidebar switches between Dashboard, Research, Draft, Review and Matters, and holds your recent research threads and projects. Your firm name and login are at the bottom of the sidebar, which opens Account. Lex helps lawyers work faster, but a lawyer must check everything it produces before relying on it.
